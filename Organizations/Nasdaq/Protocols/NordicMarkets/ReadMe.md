## Nasdaq Nordic Markets


Nasdaq Nordic Markets consolidator — the GENIUM Market Info system that collects and disseminates, on the Genium Consolidated Feed (GCF), reference and real-time market data across the Nasdaq Nordic and Baltic equities, derivatives, fixed income, commodities and indexes markets.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ConsolidatedFeed](ConsolidatedFeed.md) | MarketData | Nasdaq Nordic Consolidated Tip Market Data |
