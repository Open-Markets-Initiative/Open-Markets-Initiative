## Nasdaq Nordic Derivatives


Nasdaq Nordic derivatives, fixed income, currency and commodities markets (the Nasdaq Stockholm, Copenhagen and Helsinki derivatives and fixed income markets and Nasdaq Commodities) trading on the Genium INET platform.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [DepthOfBook](DepthOfBook.md) | MarketData | Nasdaq Nordic Genium INET ITCH and GLIMPSE |
| [MarketData](MarketData.md) | MarketData | Nasdaq Nordic Genium INET AMD |
| [OrderEntry](OrderEntry.md) | OrderEntry | Nasdaq Nordic Genium INET FIX |
