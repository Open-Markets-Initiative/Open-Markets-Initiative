## NtxEquities Rash: Nasdaq Ntx Rash

Routing And Special Handling (RASH) ascii order entry protocol for entering orders with routing, discretion, pegging and reserve instructions, cancelling orders and receiving executions on Nasdaq Texas.

### Transport

Tcp carrying fixed length ascii RASH messages over SoupTcp or SoupBinTcp, inbound messages unsequenced and outbound messages sequenced.
