## Nasdaq Texas


Nasdaq Texas US cash equities exchange — successor to the Nasdaq Boston (BX) Stock Market following the relocation of operations to Texas.

| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bbo](Bbo.md) | MarketData | Nasdaq TX Top Of Book Quotation Data |

| [Drop](Drop.md) | DropCopy | Nasdaq Ntx Drop |

| [LastSale](LastSale.md) | MarketData | Nasdaq Texas Last Sale, formerly Nasdaq BX Last Sale (BLS) |
| [MatchView](MatchView.md) | MarketData | Nasdaq Texas View Of Away Market Best Bid And Offer |
