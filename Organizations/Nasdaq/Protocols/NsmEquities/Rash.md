## NsmEquities Rash: Nasdaq Nsm Rash

Routing And Special Handling (RASH) ascii order entry protocol for entering orders with routing, discretion, pegging and reserve instructions, cancelling orders and receiving executions on Nasdaq Stock Market.

### Transport

Tcp carrying fixed length ascii RASH messages over SoupTcp or SoupBinTcp, inbound messages unsequenced and outbound messages sequenced.
