## Nasdaq Canada


Nasdaq Canada (Nasdaq CXC Limited, formerly Chi-X Canada) Canadian cash equities marketplace operating three trading books, CXC (MIC CHIC), CX2 (MIC XCX2) and the CXD dark book (MIC XCXD), on the Nasdaq INET platform. One set of order entry and market data specifications serves all three books, which are selected by a destination field on the order and by channel on the market data feeds.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Chixmd](Chixmd.md) | MarketData | Nasdaq Canada Tcp Order And Trade Feed |
| [Chixmmd](Chixmmd.md) | MarketData | Nasdaq Canada Multicast Order And Trade Feed |
