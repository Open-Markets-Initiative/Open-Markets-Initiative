## BoxOptions Binary Retransmission: Box Options Binary Market Data Retransmission

Tcp retransmission and snapshot service of the BOX Binary Market Data Feed, replaying multicast lines by sequence range and serving dictionary, top of book and market depth snapshots.

### Transport

Retransmission and snapshot service per trading slice: login, retransmission requests by line and sequence range, and dictionary, top of book and market depth snapshots, delivered in binary blocks.
