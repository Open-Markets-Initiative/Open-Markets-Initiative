## BoxOptions Binary Multicast: Box Options Binary Multicast Market Data

Real-time BOX Options market data on the BOX Binary Market Data Feed multicast lines: instrument dictionary, opening prices, market depth, top of book quotes, trades, auctions and trading status.

### Transport

Multicast lines (Top of Book, Market Depth, Complex, Auction) on redundant feeds A and B per trading slice, one binary block per datagram.
