## Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange

Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange, a subsidiary of Vietnam Exchange and the main board of the Vietnamese market, trading cash equities, exchange traded funds, covered warrants and indices. The whole Vietnamese market moved to the KRX System, built by Korea Exchange, at go live on 5 May 2025.
