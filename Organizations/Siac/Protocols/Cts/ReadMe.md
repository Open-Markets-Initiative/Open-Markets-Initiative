## Consolidated Tape System


Consolidated last sale (trade) feed for Nyse-listed and Nyse Amex-listed securities (Tape A and Tape B), published by Siac on behalf of the Consolidated Tape Association. Aggregates trades from participating U.S. Stock Exchanges and the FINRA Alternative Display Facility, and disseminates Consolidated/Participant High, Low, Last, Volume and LULD Price Bands.
