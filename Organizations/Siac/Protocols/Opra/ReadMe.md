## Options Price Reporting Authority


OPRA is the Securities Information Processor for U.S. listed equity options market data, distributing consolidated last sale and quotation data from all participant options exchanges. Operated by Siac under SEC oversight.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Opra](Opra.md) | MarketData | Siac Consolidated Options Market Data Feed |
