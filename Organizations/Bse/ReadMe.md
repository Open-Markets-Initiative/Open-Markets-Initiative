## BSE Limited

Asia's oldest stock exchange, founded in Bombay in 1875 and now trading equities, equity derivatives, currency derivatives, and commodity derivatives. Its market and trading interfaces run on a licensed deployment of the T7 platform, giving BSE the same Eobi, Emdi, and Eti interface family used by Eurex.
