## EEX Derivatives


The European Energy Exchange derivatives market for power, natural gas, emissions, freight and agricultural contracts, traded on the Deutsche Boerse T7 Derivatives Trading System.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Emdi](Emdi.md) | MarketData | EEX T7 Price Level Market Data |
| [Eobi](Eobi.md) | MarketData | EEX T7 Order Book Market Data |
| [Mdi](Mdi.md) | MarketData | EEX T7 Netted Market Data |
| [Rdi](Rdi.md) | MarketData | EEX T7 Reference Data |
