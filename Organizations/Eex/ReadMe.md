## European Energy Exchange

European Energy Exchange AG, part of Deutsche Boerse Group, trading power and gas spot and derivatives, emissions, freight and agricultural products on the Deutsche Boerse T7 platform. EEX Group also includes EPEX SPOT, Nodal Exchange and the Power Exchange Central Europe.
