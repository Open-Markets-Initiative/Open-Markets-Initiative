## Qatar Stock Exchange


The cash market of the Qatar Stock Exchange, covering Qatari equities, treasury bills, bonds and sukuk and indices. The exchange ran the NYSE Technologies Universal Trading Platform, whose market data feed is specified jointly for Euronext and Qatar Exchange and separates the two by service id, before migrating to LSEG Technology.
