## Elasticia


Elasticia is the Nordic Growth Market trading system, whose market model and Fix protocol carry its name. Members reach it through the Elasticia Fix Protocol, Fix Latest application messages over Fixt 1.1 sessions offering an order entry service and a market data service over Tcp, encoded as Fix tag value or as Fast, published as a Fix Orchestra repository with QuickFix dictionaries and Fast templates.
