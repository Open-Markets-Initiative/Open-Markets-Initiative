## Moscow Exchange

Moscow Exchange, operated by PJSC Moscow Exchange MICEX-RTS, running two trading platforms: ASTS for equities, bonds, FX and the money market, and Spectra for derivatives. Market data is published as SIMBA, a Simple Binary Encoding feed, and as the legacy FAST multicast feed; order entry is TWIME, FIX and the legacy Plaza-2 replication API.
