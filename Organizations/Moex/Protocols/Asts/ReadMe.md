## ASTS


Asts is the Moscow Exchange trading system for the equity and bond market, the foreign exchange market and the money market, the counterpart of the Spectra platform that carries derivatives. Its market data is published as the Fix Fast multicast platform, whose feeds carry the equity and bond tables and the currency tables under templates of their own, and as Simba, and its order entry is Fix, Twime and the Bridge interface.
