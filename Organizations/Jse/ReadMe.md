## JSE Limited

JSE Limited, the Johannesburg Stock Exchange, is the largest securities exchange in Africa. It operates an equity market, an equity derivatives market, a currency derivatives market, a commodity derivatives market, an interest rate market and a bond market, and runs the Namibian Stock Exchange order book alongside its own. Its markets trade on the Integrated Trading and Clearing platform.
