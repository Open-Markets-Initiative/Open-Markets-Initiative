## Integrated Trading and Clearing


Itac is the JSE trading and information solution, built on Millennium IT, that carries the exchange's markets on one platform: the equity market including the Namibian Stock Exchange order book, equity derivatives, currency derivatives, commodity derivatives, the interest rate market and the bond market. Participants reach it through a common set of gateways rather than one per market — Fix trading, post trade and drop copy, the basic and enhanced native trading gateways, and the Mitch and Fast market data feeds — with the market and instrument selected per order rather than per connection.
