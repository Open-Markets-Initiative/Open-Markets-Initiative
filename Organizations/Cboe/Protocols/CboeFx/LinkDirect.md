## CboeFx Link Direct: Cboe Fx Link Direct

Cboe FX Link Direct, which had its own order types until the Operating Procedures deleted its section and order types.

### Overview

The Cboe FX Operating Procedures added Link Direct order types alongside the Full Amount and Cboe FX Central order types in their August 1, 2022 revision. A later revision deleted the Link Direct section and its order types. No Link Direct protocol specification is available.
