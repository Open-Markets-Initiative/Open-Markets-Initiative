## Singapore Exchange

Singapore Exchange (SGX) is the primary securities and derivatives exchange of Singapore, operating equities, fixed income, currency and commodity derivatives markets including the SGX Reach securities trading system, the Titan derivatives platform, and the Titan OTC trade registration service.
