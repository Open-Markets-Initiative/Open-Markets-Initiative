## TitanDt Depth Of Book: Depth Of Book

Market by order depth of book feed for the Sgx Titan derivatives markets, publishing every order and quote in every identified order book together with order book directory, combination order book directory, tick size table, order book state, trade and equilibrium price information.

### Transport

Udp multicast framed by MoldUdp64, with a separate rewind service for retransmission of missed sequence numbers.
