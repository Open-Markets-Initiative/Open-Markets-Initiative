## Shanghai Stock Exchange Securities Market


The Shanghai Stock Exchange securities market covers stocks (A and B shares), funds and public REITs, bonds and repos, options and indices. Market data for it is disseminated by the Market Data Gateway (MDGW) under the IS120 interface specification.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Mdgw](Mdgw.md) | MarketData | Market Data Gateway |
