## Canadian Securities Exchange

Canadian Securities Exchange, operated by CNSX Markets Inc., trading Canadian cash equities across the CSE and CSE2 order books. Order entry is FIX 4.2; market data is a multicast broadcast feed carrying Level 1 best quotes, Level 2 depth, trades, committed orders, bulletins and market statistics.
