## Bolsa Mexicana de Valores Market Data


BMV market data products published over INTRA Multicast: capital market, MexDer derivatives, money market, indices, added value, analytics, MOC, instrument catalogs and the Consolidated Feed of BMV and BIVA trades, with Tcp replay and snapshot recovery.
