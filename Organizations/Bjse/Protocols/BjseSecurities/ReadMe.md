## Beijing Stock Exchange


Beijing Stock Exchange's trading platforms, covering stocks, convertible bonds and the fixed income platform's spot bond trading. Market data is published through the market quotation gateway (BJQG) that the exchange shares with the National Equities Exchange and Quotations, under interface specifications the two issue jointly.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MulticastMarketData](MulticastMarketData.md) | MarketData | Bjse Multicast Market Data |
| [TcpMarketData](TcpMarketData.md) | MarketData | Bjse Tcp Market Data |
