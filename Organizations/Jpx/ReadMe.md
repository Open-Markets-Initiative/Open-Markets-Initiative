## Japan Exchange Group

The exchange group was formed by the merger of Tokyo Stock Exchange Group, Inc. (ja) and Osaka Securities Exchange Co.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **JPX protocol specifications are not published in this repository.** Japan Exchange Group distributes its wire-level interface specifications — arrowhead FLEX market data, OSE J-GATE ITCH/OUCH order entry, and the connectivity manuals and message references that accompany them — to trading participants and authorised vendors under JPX's participant and vendor agreements. Those documents are confidential and may not be disclosed or redistributed. No JPX specification document, and no JPX trademark or logo, is held in this repository or will be published here. Access requests should be directed to JPX: https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/
