## Osaka Securities Exchange


Osaka Securities Exchange derivatives market operated by Japan Exchange Group on the Genium Inet trading platform, listing futures and options including the Nikkei 225 complex.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [GeniumInet.Itch](GeniumInet.Itch.md) | MarketData | Osaka Securities Exchange Itch Market Data |
| [GeniumInet.Ouch](GeniumInet.Ouch.md) | OrderEntry | Osaka Securities Exchange Ouch Order Entry |
