## SIX Swiss Exchange

SIX Swiss Exchange, operated by SIX Group AG, trading Swiss cash equities, exchange traded funds, bonds, structured products and derivatives on the SWXess platform built on X-stream INET. Market data is published over the ITCH Market Data Interface and the MDDX consolidated feed. SIX Group also operates BME in Spain.
