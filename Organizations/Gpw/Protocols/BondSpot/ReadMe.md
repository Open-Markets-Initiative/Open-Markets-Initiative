## BondSpot


BondSpot, the fixed income market of the Warsaw Stock Exchange group, operating the Treasury BondSpot Poland wholesale market in treasury bonds and bills, the Catalyst regulated market and an alternative trading system. Appendix 2.3 of the market data specification gives it three stock exchange codes, 986 for the non exchange regulated market, 988 for Treasury BondSpot Poland and 989 for the alternative trading system, and appendix 2.8 three segment identifiers, RPWC BOSP and TBSP.
