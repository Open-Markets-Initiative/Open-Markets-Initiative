## Warsaw Stock Exchange


The Warsaw Stock Exchange, trading Polish cash equities on the main market and on the NewConnect alternative market, Catalyst bonds, structured products, exchange traded products and financial derivatives, on the NYSE Technologies Universal Trading Platform. Appendix 2.8 of the market data specification gives the exchange eleven segment identifiers, XWAR for the main market, XNCO for NewConnect, WBON WMTF and WBCL for Catalyst, WDER for derivatives, WETP for exchange traded products and WIND for indices.
