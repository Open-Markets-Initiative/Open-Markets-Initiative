## MDCS Realtime Market Data


Koscom Market Data Service (MDCS) realtime UDP multicast distribution of Korea Exchange (KRX) market data. Carries KRX KOSPI, KOSDAQ, KONEX, K-OTC, Derivatives, Bonds, and Index data plus Koscom-originated reference feeds. All messages are ASCII fixed-width text framed in UDP datagrams.
