## Italian Derivatives Market


Idem is the Italian Derivatives Market of Borsa Italiana, listing futures and options on equity indices, single stocks and commodities. It runs on the Sola trading platform and is reached over Fix or the native Sola Access Information Language (Sail) protocol.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OrderEntry](OrderEntry.md) | OrderEntry | Sola Fix Order Entry |
