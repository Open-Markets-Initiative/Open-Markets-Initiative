## Athens Exchange Securities Market


The securities market of the Athens Exchange, trading Greek cash equities, bonds, exchange traded funds, warrants, rights and repos on the Oasis trading system. The Information Dissemination Service carries it alongside the derivatives market on one stream, telling the two apart by the Venue ID of the packet header and the financial product by the Message Subcategory, which names bonds, exchange traded funds and their indicative net asset values, stock indices, stocks and rights, warrants and repos.
