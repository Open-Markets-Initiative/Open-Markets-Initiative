## OASIS


Oasis is the Athens Exchange trading system, carrying the securities and derivatives markets on one platform and running on Linux since 2024. Its market data leaves it as the ascii Information Dissemination Service and as the Market Data Feed Service, a Fix 5.0 Sp2 feed Fast encoded over Udp multicast and Tcp, both serving every venue and instrument type the platform trades.
