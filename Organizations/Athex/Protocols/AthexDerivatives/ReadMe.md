## Athens Exchange Derivatives Market


The derivatives market of the Athens Exchange, formerly the Athens Derivatives Exchange and merged into the exchange in 2002, trading index futures and options, single stock futures and options and standard combinations on the Oasis trading system. The Information Dissemination Service carries it alongside the securities market on one stream, and its Message Subcategory names options, futures and standard combinations. The market data specification names no derivatives Market Identification Code of its own; XADE is the Iso 10383 code for this market.
