## Aquis Stock Exchange


Aquis Stock Exchange (AQSE), the UK equity and debt market operated by Aquis, with Main Board, MTF trading and Growth Market segments (MICs AQSL, AQSD, AQST, AQSN, AQSG and AQSF) on which members trade against market maker quotes and orders and report off book trades.
