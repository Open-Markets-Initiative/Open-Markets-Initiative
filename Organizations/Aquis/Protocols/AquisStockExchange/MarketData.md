## AquisStockExchange Market Data: Aquis Stock Exchange FIX 4.4 Market Data

Fix 4.4 market data for Aquis Stock Exchange: market maker quotes by subscription (Market Data Request, Snapshot Full Refresh and Incremental Refresh), trade capture reports by subscription, and News messages for market states and security and participant suspensions.

### Overview

AQSE disseminates market data relating to Trade Capture reports, Quotes, market states, security suspensions and restorations and FIX participant suspensions and restorations using the FIX 4.4 protocol. Continuous trading market data is in the Aquis Stock Exchange Multicast Market Data Technical Specification.

The security identifier on quotes and trade capture reports is an ISIN; trade reports sent to subscribers who did not originate them are anonymised.

### Transport

Fix 4.4 sessions; network, address and login details are provided by the AQSE connectivity team.
