## NETS Trading System


NETS is the trading system of the National Stock Exchange of Australia, publishing public market data over ITCH and accepting order management over FIX.


### Protocols


| Protocol | Type | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MarketDataFeed](MarketDataFeed.md) | MarketData | Nsx Australia Equities Market Data |
