## Hkex China Connect


Hkex China Connect is the mutual market access facility through which Exchange Participants trade eligible securities listed on the Shanghai and Shenzhen stock exchanges. Northbound order flow reaches the Sse and Szse order books through the China Connect Central Gateway (CCCG), which exposes the FIX trading and drop copy interfaces. Not an order book exchange; matching happens on the mainland venues, whose market data Hkex republishes as the Sse and Szse China Connect OMD feeds.
