## Taiwan Futures Exchange

The Taiwan Futures Exchange is the sole futures and options exchange in Taiwan. Founded in 1997 and beginning trading in 1998 with the Taiwan Stock Exchange Capitalization Weighted Stock Index futures contract, it lists index futures and options, single stock futures and options, interest rate futures, commodity futures and foreign exchange futures. Taifex operates regular and after hours trading sessions alongside a Flex trading service for tailored contracts, and clears its own contracts through an integrated clearing house.
