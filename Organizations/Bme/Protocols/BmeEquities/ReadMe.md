## BME Equities


The equities environment of the BME trading system, which the binary interface specifications call RV, trading Spanish cash equities, exchange traded funds, warrants and certificates on the SIBE platform. Table 2 of the BMEGate Codification Tables gives the environment the operating MIC BMEX and nine segment MICs, XMAD for Bolsa de Madrid, XBAR for Barcelona, XBIL for Bilbao, XVAL for Valencia, MABX for Mercado Alternativo Bursatil, XLAT for Latibex, GROW for BME Growth, SCLE for BME ScaleUp and DMAD for the Bolsa de Madrid dark book.
