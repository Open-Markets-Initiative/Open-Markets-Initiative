## BME Fixed Income


The fixed income environment of the BME trading system, which the binary interface specifications call RF, trading Spanish public and private debt. Table 2 of the BMEGate Codification Tables gives the environment the operating MIC BMEX with segment MICs XDRF for AIAF, MARF for the Mercado Alternativo de Renta Fija, MERF for the Mercado Electronico de Renta Fija, SEND for the retail debt platform and SBAR and SBIL for the Barcelona and Bilbao fixed income markets, and the separate operating MIC XNAF for SENAF, the wholesale government debt platform.
