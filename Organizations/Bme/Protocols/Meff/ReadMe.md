## MEFF


The derivatives environment of the BME trading system, which the binary interface specifications call DE, and the reference market for options and futures on the IBEX 35 index and on Spanish single stocks, dividend futures and xRolling FX futures. Table 2 of the BMEGate Codification Tables gives the environment the operating MIC BMEX with segment MICs XMRV for financial derivatives, XMPW for energy derivatives and XMFX for foreign exchange derivatives.
