## FENICS

FENICS, part of BGC Partners, providing FX trading and market data. FENICS FX covers spot, non deliverable forwards, options and electronic trading, and FENICS Market Data publishes FX option volatility surfaces, forward and NDF curves and benchmark data including the benchmark cash on the run US Treasury feed.
