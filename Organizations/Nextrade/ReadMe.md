## Nextrade

Nextrade is the first alternative trading system licensed in South Korea, authorized by the Financial Services Commission to operate a multilateral trading facility for KOSPI and KOSDAQ listed equities and exchange traded products in competition with the Korea Exchange. It extends the Korean trading day with pre-market and after-market sessions and introduces midpoint order types alongside the conventional limit and market order book.
