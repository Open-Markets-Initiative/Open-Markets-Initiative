## Shenzhen Stock Exchange

Shenzhen Stock Exchange, trading Chinese cash equities, funds and exchange traded funds, bonds, and ETF options on the 300ETF and ChiNext ETF. Market data is published over the BINARY and STEP market data feeds and the multicast distribution protocol, with English editions issued by Shenzhen Securities Information.
