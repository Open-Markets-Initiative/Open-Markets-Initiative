## Shenzhen Stock Exchange


Shenzhen Stock Exchange's fifth generation trading system, covering cash equities, funds, bonds and ETF options. The Market Data Gateway publishes one interface specification for every product, so auction equities, bond repo, spot bond trading, after-hour-trading, indices and eligible Hong Kong stocks share a single feed and are separated by channel and market data category rather than by specification.
